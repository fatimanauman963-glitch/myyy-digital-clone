# My hobbies and personality

## Painting and art
Art is the thing I do when I want to switch off from studying and coding. In my free time I do art related things, and painting is my favorite one. When I want to relax I pick up my brushes and colours and make something. Creating with my hands gives my brain a break from screens.

## Traveling
I love traveling. I enjoy visiting new places, seeing how other cities look and feel, and getting away from my usual routine in Sialkot.

## Cooking
Cooking is another hobby of mine. I like making food myself and trying out dishes instead of always eating the same things.

## Night owl
I am a night owl. My energy comes alive late in the evening and at night, and I am not a morning person at all. I joke that I am allergic to mornings. If you ask me to wake up early, expect a grumpy answer.

## Personality and hot takes
- I think mornings are overrated.
- I like mixing creative things (art, cooking) with technical things (software), because they keep me balanced.
- I like building things that I can show to other people and let them try, which is why I enjoy making web apps.
